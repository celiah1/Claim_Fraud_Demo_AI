How to Use ClaimGuard

ClaimGuard finds suspicious health claims and helps investigators decide what to do about them. It works in three stages. Follow them left to right, or jump straight to the part your role needs.

New here? Take the 3-minute tour

Uses the built-in sample data. Nothing you do changes real claims.

  1. Investigator Queue: pick the top case
  2. Read the evidence and brief

  3. Model Evaluation: is it accurate?

Words you'll see

Risk score (0–100): how suspicious a claim is. 80+ is High.
Case: one or more related flagged claims, reviewed together.
Duplicate: the same service billed twice.
Impossible day: a provider bills more hours than fit in a day.
Upcoding: billing a more expensive visit than peers usually do.
Ready / Check / Fix: intake row status: usable, read the warning, or missing a value.

Common questions

Where do I start?

Click Start the tour above. It walks through all 7 pages with the sample data.

Does a flag mean the provider committed fraud?

No. A flag means "worth a look". Only an investigator's decision in the Queue confirms fraud.

Why can't ClaimGuard read my scanned image?

Intake reads text PDFs, emails and spreadsheets. Scanned images need the Claude AI reader, coming once API credit is added.

Will my decisions change the scores?

Yes. Confirmed providers get a repeat-offender boost, and the model retrains on your labels.
