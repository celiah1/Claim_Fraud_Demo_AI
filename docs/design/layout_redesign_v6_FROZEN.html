How to Use ClaimGuard

ClaimGuard screens health claims with explainable rules and helps investigators decide which ones need a closer look. Follow the workflow below; click any step to open it.

The workflow

From claim documents to a recorded outcome and leadership reporting. Hover a step for details; click to open it.

Common questions

Click a question to expand it.

Where do I start?

Click Review a sample duplicate case above. It opens a case with the evidence, checklist and outcome form. Or click any step in the workflow.

Do I need to upload anything to try it?

No. Sample data (8,512 synthetic claims, 1 Jul – 1 Oct 2026) is already loaded.

Does a flag mean the provider committed fraud?

No. A flag means "worth a review". Reviewers record outcomes such as "Billing or coding issue substantiated"; formal fraud findings stay with SIU.

What does the review priority (0–100) mean?

How strongly the rules suggest a review: 80+ priority review, 50–79 routine review, below 50 no additional review. It is not a probability of fraud.

What are the four flag reasons?

Repeated claim (exact duplicate), near-repeated claim (±1 day, ±10%), unusual billed hours in one day, and visit level above specialty peers.

How do I record an outcome?

Open a case, go to the Record outcome tab, choose an outcome, a reason and at least one evidence reference. A substantiated outcome also needs an amount, or "Amount pending".

Why does a duplicate need payment history?

Two matching submissions show there is something to review. Only the payment records show whether money was actually overpaid.

Can I undo an outcome?

Yes. Reverse it with a reason. The reversal is added to the case history; nothing is deleted.

Where is the case brief?

Click 📄 Case brief in the case header for a short summary: why it was flagged, key evidence, legitimate explanations and the first step.

Will my decisions change the scores?

Not automatically. Any change to rules or the model needs independent validation and approval first.

Is this real data?

No. Everything in this demo is synthetic, including the accuracy figures.

Does any data leave the system?

Not today. The case brief and Ask your data are rule-based and run locally. AI features stay off until their data flow is reviewed.

Can I just ask a question?

Yes. Click ✦ Ask your data at the bottom right of any page.

Words you'll see

Billed · Flagged · Substantiated

What was charged · what a rule found unusual · what a reviewer substantiated with evidence.

Outcomes

No issue found · Billing or coding issue substantiated · Insufficient evidence · Refer to SIU.

Case

One or more related flagged claims, reviewed together.

Duplicate

The same service billed twice.

Unusual billed hours

Estimated hours from billed codes and units exceed what fits in a day.

Upcoding

Billing a more expensive visit than peers usually do.

Pending reconciliation

An amount not yet confirmed by the claims system. Not the same as $0.

Ready / Check / Fix

Intake row status: usable, read the warning, or missing a value.

More detail: before citing real-world performance

Before citing real-world performance

  • Point-in-time features: some features use group statistics (peer medians) computed across the whole supplied data. For a valid test they must be computed only from claims available at scoring time; a date split alone doesn't guarantee that.
  • Later data: validate on a period after the training data, with labels from reviewed line-level findings only.
  • Approval: independent validation and sign-off before any model is used to set review priority.

How ClaimGuard Works

How ClaimGuard works technically: the pipeline, the data contract, the rules and their scores, the model, the assistant and the deployment plan for the pilot.

Pipeline

Every stage is deterministic and logged; AI steps are optional and off by default.

Every input is mapped to this schema before screening.

Rules and scores

Each rule returns a base score and a reason string. Ruleset v1.4.

RuleConditionBase score
Repeated claimsame member, provider, code, date, amount92
Near-repeated claim±1 day and ±10% amount68
Unusual billed hoursΣ units × code minutes ÷ 60 > 12 h60 + 3 per hour over 12; ≥ 85 above 16 h; max 97
Visit level above peers99215 share > leave-one-out peer median + 25 pts and z > 2, ≥ 20 visits55 + 5 × z; max 88
Review priority = max(base score of the case's lines) + optional adjustment. Routing: ≥ 80 priority · 50–79 routine · < 50 none.

✦ Ask your data

Question → intent → read-only query → answer with its working.

question: "Has Dr. Osei billed long days before?"
intent:   provider_long_days(provider = PRV-1031)
query:    SELECT service_date, SUM(units*minutes)/60 AS hours
          FROM claims JOIN cpt_reference USING (cpt_code)
          WHERE provider_id = 'PRV-1031'
          GROUP BY 1 HAVING hours > 12
guards:   read-only · member IDs only · no guilt judgements
Rule-based today (src/assistant.py). A Claude version would translate questions into the same guarded, read-only queries.
