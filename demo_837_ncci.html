Insurer File Import & NCCI Check

Load the electronic claim file an insurer already receives from hospitals and clinics. ClaimGuard turns it into claim rows, then checks every claim against Medicare's list of codes that must not be billed together.

📄 What is an 837 file?

The standard electronic format every US insurer receives claims in. It's coded text, not a spreadsheet. Reading it directly means an insurer can drop in real claims on day one, with no conversion step.

📘 What are NCCI edits?

A free list published by Medicare of procedure pairs that should not be billed together, because one already includes the other. Billing both is called unbundling: charging twice for the same work.
