For claims investigators & payment-integrity teams

Decide which flagged claims need a closer look.

ClaimGuard screens every claim with explainable rules and puts the most unusual ones in a queue. For each case it shows why it was flagged, what to check next, and where to record your decision.

 

Every case answers three questions

1
Why was this flagged?The rule, the comparison, and the exact claim rows behind it.
2
What should I check next?A short checklist of records and comparisons for this flag reason.
3
What did I decide?Confirm, clear or refer, with a note. Every decision can be undone.
