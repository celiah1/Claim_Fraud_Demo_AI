How to Use ClaimGuard

ClaimGuard screens health claims with explainable rules and helps investigators decide which ones need a closer look. It works in three stages. Follow them left to right, or jump straight to the part your role needs.

For claims investigators & payment-integrity teams

See it in 1 minute: review a real-looking case

Opens the top flagged case from the sample data. You'll see why it was flagged, what to check, and where to record a decision. No upload needed.

   

Every case answers three questions

1
Why was this flagged?The rule, the comparison and the exact claim rows.
2
What should I check next?A checklist for that flag reason.
3
Where do I record my decision?Confirm, clear or refer, with a note. Undo any time.

Words you'll see

Risk score (0–100): how unusual a case is compared with peers. 0–49 normal, 50–79 review, 80+ high. A screening score, not a probability of fraud.
Billed · Flagged · Confirmed: what was charged · what a rule found unusual · what a reviewer confirmed.
Case: one or more related flagged claims, reviewed together.
Duplicate: the same service billed twice.
Impossible day: a provider bills more hours than fit in a day.
Upcoding: billing a more expensive visit than peers usually do.
Ready / Check / Fix: intake row status: usable, read the warning, or missing a value.

Common questions

Where do I start?

Click Review a sample case above for the 1-minute version, or Full 7-step tour to walk through every page with the sample data.

Does a flag mean the provider committed fraud?

No. A flag means "worth a look". Only an investigator's decision in the Queue confirms fraud.

Why can't ClaimGuard read my scanned image?

Intake reads text PDFs, emails and spreadsheets. Scanned images need the Claude AI reader, coming once API credit is added.

Will my decisions change the scores?

Yes. Confirmed providers get a repeat-offender boost, and the model retrains on your labels.

Can I just ask a question?

Yes. Click ✦ Ask your data at the bottom right of any page. The assistant answers from the loaded claims, shows how it got the answer, and suggests questions for the page you're on.

Is this real data?

No. Everything in this demo is synthetic, including the accuracy figures. Sample decisions can be undone.

What your decisions change

  • Confirmed → that provider's other open cases get +10 risk (repeat offender)
  • Not a problem → case closes; counts as a false alarm for the rule
  • Referred → stays open for the audit team
  • All decisions become training labels for the model
